# Cell_Segmentation

Neurological disorders, such as Alzheimer's and brain tumors, are a major global health concern. Assessing the effectiveness of treatments for these conditions often involves examining neuronal cells through light microscopy. However, accurately identifying individual neuronal cells in microscopic images is challenging. Improved computer vision techniques can facilitate this process and potentially lead to new drug discoveries.

Existing solutions have limited accuracy, particularly when dealing with neuronal cells, which have unique and irregular shapes. That is why I am trying to build ML model, which can robustly perform this task and assist doctors.
